# Privacy policy for JAX DNA

JAX DNA is a research project of the Jacksonville Civic Council, a nonprofit civic organization in Jacksonville, Florida. It measures how people who post publicly about Jacksonville feel about the city. This page explains what the project collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

We read public posts that mention Jacksonville from platforms that offer an official interface for doing so (currently Bluesky; Reddit and X when access is granted). For each post we keep the text, the time it was posted, the platform, the subreddit or search term it came from, the number of likes or upvotes, and a link to the post.

## What we do not collect

We do not store usernames, handles, display names, profile information, follower lists, or any other data about the person who wrote a post. Links to Bluesky posts use the account's technical identifier rather than the handle. We do not combine this data with any other data set, and we never attempt to identify or profile individual people.

## How we use it

Each post is scored for sentiment (positive, neutral, negative) by automated models. Scores are then averaged by day and by topic (for example, downtown, utilities, schools, traffic, housing). Only these averages are published. The published dashboard shows example posts with their public links, as any reader of the platform could see them.

We do not use the data to infer anything about individual users, including political views, health, or other personal characteristics. We do not sell or share the raw data. We do not use it to train machine learning models or to target advertising.

## Deleted posts

Posts that their authors later delete are removed from our records when we re-check them, and are excluded from published examples.

## Retention

Raw post text is kept only as long as the project needs it to compute and audit the published index. Aggregate daily and topic figures are kept indefinitely.
