Sources and method

Each post is scored from minus one (very negative) to plus one (very positive). VADER is a word-list model; RoBERTa is a language model trained on tweets. Daily means are smoothed over seven days, weighted by how many posts each day had. Days with too few posts are dropped. Topics come from keyword rules and are meant to be edited. This is an exploratory tool: the numbers describe people who post publicly about Jacksonville, not all residents.
